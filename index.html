A QUICK NOTE ON SAFETY

Good finds.
Safe handovers.

CampusFind is a community board, not an identity verification service. A few smart steps can help protect you and your belongings.

  • ✓ Keep student IDs, phone numbers, and private details out of public descriptions.
  • ✓ Ask the claimant to describe a hidden detail before handing over an item.
  • ✓ Arrange collection at a staffed campus desk or another public place.
